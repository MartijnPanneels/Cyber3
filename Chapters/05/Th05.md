**What different "rules" exist when talking about a backup strategy? Explain what you need to take into consideration.**

The 3-2-1 rule:

3 Copies of data, (the data used in production and 2 other disctinct copies).
2 Different types of media, local hard drive, a NAS, the cloud.
1 offsite copy, one copy must be seperated from the other copies. 'If the house burns down'.

There are variations like the 3-2-1-1-0

The same as 3-2-1 version but with 1 offline copy with 0 errors after verification.
